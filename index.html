01 / About us

Curiosity is the
starting point.

Deep Learning @ MIT brings students together to understand how deep learning works and what we can do with it. We’re a community for asking questions, working through ideas, and turning what we learn into projects.

02 / What we do

Read. Discuss. Build.

[ 01 ]

Explore the fundamentals

Work through the concepts behind neural networks, from first principles to the models shaping the field.

[ 02 ]

Unpack the research

Read papers together, compare perspectives, and make room for the questions that lead to deeper understanding.

[ 03 ]

Learn by building

Try out ideas in code. Collaborate on experiments and small projects that connect theory with practice.
